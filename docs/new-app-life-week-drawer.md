# Life week drawer for a new app

Keep the selected week visible without letting its details cover the life grid.
The resting drawer shows the week number and age in one compact row.
Tap the header to expand or collapse it; tap the expanded details to open the full week.
Selecting a grid square reveals details briefly, then retracts them with a gentle transition.

Give the drawer a surface color and defined edge that are clearly distinct from both the page and bottom navigation in light and dark themes.
Check the resting and expanded states on a phone at normal and enlarged text sizes.
